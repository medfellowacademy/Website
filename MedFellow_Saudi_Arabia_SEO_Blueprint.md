The fellowship certificate is recognised as a structured specialty credential by private hospitals across Saudi Arabia when submitted alongside your MBBS and postgraduate degrees. The practical effect is that the certificate strengthens private hospital applications directly and supports public hospital applications as part of the broader credential set.
